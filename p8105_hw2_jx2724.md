The cleaned subway dataset contains information on subway entrances and
exits, including the subway line, station name, station latitude and
longitude, routes served, entrance type, entry availability, vending
availability, and ADA compliance. I cleaned the data by standardizing
variable names, retaining only the variables required for this analysis,
and converting the `entry` variable from character values (`YES` and
`NO`) to a logical variable.

The resulting dataset has 1868 rows and 19 columns. The data are not
fully tidy because route information is stored across multiple variables
(`route1` through `route11`) rather than in a single route variable.

``` r
mci_baseline =
```

``` r
    is.na(age_at_onset) | age_at_onset > current_age
  )
```

After importing the baseline demographic data, I cleaned the variable
names, recoded sex and APOE4 carrier status as categorical variables,
converted MCI onset age to numeric, and removed participants who already
had MCI at baseline. The cleaned baseline dataset contains 479
participants, of whom 93 developed MCI during follow-up. The average
baseline age is approximately 65.03 years. Among women in the study, 30%
are APOE4 carriers.
